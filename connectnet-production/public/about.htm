Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common ConnectNet questions.

Go to the "Get Wi-Fi" or "Purchase" page, select your location and package, enter your details, choose a payment method and complete your payment.

First confirm that your payment was successful and that your Wi-Fi voucher has not expired. Try disconnecting and reconnecting to the ConnectNet hotspot. If the problem continues, contact support with your payment reference.

Turn on Wi-Fi on your phone or computer, select the ConnectNet network and open your browser. The hotspot login page should appear. Enter the username and password provided after your purchase.

Device access depends on the Wi-Fi package and hotspot settings. If your package supports another device, follow the instructions provided with your voucher or contact support.

Go to the Login page and select "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email or phone number and follow the password reset instructions.

Log into your ConnectNet account to view your active package, remaining time or data where that information is supported by the hotspot system.

Quick Troubleshooting

Try these steps before contacting support.

1

Turn Wi-Fi off and on

Disable Wi-Fi on your device for a few seconds, then enable it again.

2

Forget the network

Forget the ConnectNet network and reconnect using the correct Wi-Fi credentials.

3

Open your browser

After connecting to Wi-Fi, open a browser to allow the hotspot login page to appear.

4

Check your package

Confirm that your package has not expired and that you purchased it for the correct location.

5

Restart your device

Restart your phone or computer and try connecting again.

6

Contact support

If the issue remains, send us your location, phone number and payment reference.
